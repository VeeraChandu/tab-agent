How it works

Tab Agent reads the active tab's content and interactive elements, sends them to the model along with your task, and executes whatever action the model chooses (click, type, scroll, navigate, switch tabs) directly on the browser — no MCP server, no proxy, no extra setup.

Because the agent can click and type on pages on your behalf, review what it's about to do in the side panel log, and use Stop at any time.
